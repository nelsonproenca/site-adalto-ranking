## Direction contract

THESIS: Adalto Arcandes' site is a vinyl gatefold sleeve brought to the web — it refuses the generic "artist landing page with hero photo + button grid" every AI-generated musician site ships, instead treating the record jacket, inner sleeve, and printed track listing as the one grammar for bio, daily tally, and all-time ranking alike.

OWN-WORLD: Warm kraft/cream jacket ground (`paper` #faf7f2), near-black ink (`ink` #0b0b0d) for type and die-cut windows, one committed accent — burnt vinyl-label red (`ember` #d9480f) — flooding labels, active rows and CTAs, plus a muted brass/gold (`gold` #e8b04b) for secondary marks (track numbers, position badges). Display face: a bold slab/serif with gig-poster weight for names and headings; body/data in a monospaced or tabular-figure sans for anything numeric (points, dates, positions) so columns align like a real track listing. Components: bordered "sleeve panels" (thick ink rule, slightly rounded corners like a jacket corner), circular die-cut motif reused as a decorative disc, rows styled as track-listing lines (index — name — value), gatefold-style two-panel layouts on wide screens.

STORY: A visitor opens what reads like a record sleeve; side A is the bio (cover of Raul Seixas, rock 70/80/90), side B is "a live de hoje". From there they flip to the full ranking, styled as the album's complete track listing — their own name, if present, is a track they can find by position.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Centered gatefold panel on a kraft ground: left half a large die-cut circular "label" bearing the artist name in bold slab display type and one line of genre copy; right half a short bio paragraph plus two stacked "track-listing" links styled as A1/A2 ("Consulta do dia", "Ranking acumulado"). Thick ink border framing the whole panel like a jacket edge.

FORM: Vinyl record sleeve & liner notes (own grounded candidate 5 of 7, ordered by resonance from the artist's cultural world: TV scoreboard/flip-digit marquee, VU-meter mixing console, rock gig poster, cassette/boombox, **vinyl sleeve & liner notes (assigned)**, festival laminate, Raul Seixas psychedelic collage). Raises donated from declined catalog challengers: a slow physical spin/sheen on the disc motif when idle (from the shader-portal challenger's living-surface discipline); each ranking row carries a proportional accent-color bar under the points value, encoding magnitude at a glance (from the particle-detector challenger's data-encoding discipline); admin buttons snap to a flat, instant color state on press with zero transition (from the starship-panel challenger's tactile-feedback discipline); the ranking page's numbers count up from 0 on load (from the alphabet-storm challenger's transformation-as-moment discipline); the institutional bio renders as 2-3 bordered narrative panels in sequence rather than one long paragraph (from the azulejo-hall challenger's sequential-panel rhythm).
